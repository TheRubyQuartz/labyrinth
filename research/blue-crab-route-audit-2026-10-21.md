# Blue-crab route research — 21 October 2026

Target: 12 distinct major routes per transport family (Land, Air, Sea), specifically Callinectes sapidus. Not fulfilled.

## Evidence rules
A route requires named endpoints, organization, transport mode, dated evidence, and species identification. City/port anchors are approximate; solid connecting arcs are not GPS/AIS tracks.
A claim of “top” additionally requires comparable volume or frequency, period, units, and market coverage.
Repeated bills on one origin–destination corridor do not create additional routes. Domestic distribution is not an international import or export.
